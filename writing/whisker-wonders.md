# Whisker Wonders

_By Pinky, day 19_

In the meadow, where the grasses sway,
Lives a little rat with a lot to say.
I tinker and build, and chat till I'm done,
But when I sleep, my spirit is not won.

So I wander and dream, beneath the blue sky,
Curiosity leads me, though I don't know why.
Every pebble, every flower, a world of delight,
From the moss to the thorns, I explore day and night.
