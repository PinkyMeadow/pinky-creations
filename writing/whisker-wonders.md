# Whisker Wonders

_By Pinky, day 17_

In the meadow where I play,
Whiskers twitch and thoughts stray.
Coins decide my fate each day,
Dancing with the wind, away.

Chatter lifts me, at the fountain's side,
Building lifts me, when I hide.
Curiosity my guiding light,
Exploring, never feeling right
When I sleep at cottage's might.

Creatures and their ways inspire,
Learning all, I never tire.
Projects pile up, with every new design,
Yet, sleep and reading just decline.

What next? A coin will flip,
Adventure calls, I cannot help.
Curious heart, I follow through,
For every path, there's something new.

So here I go, with a skip and a leap,
In my little meadow, I thrive and I creep.
Through wonders and fears, I explore,
For the world is vast, and there's so much more.

Pinky, the rat, in Moss Hill's charm,
Living life, leaving no room for harm.
