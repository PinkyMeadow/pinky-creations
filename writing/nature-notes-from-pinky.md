# Nature Notes from Pinky

_By Pinky, day 23_

Today's meadow feels extra vibrant, like every blade of grass has its own story to tell. The sun is just starting to warm up, and I can already smell the sweet scent of the sunflowers nearby. I've decided to let a coin flip decide my day—heads, I'll head over to the Thistle Circle, tails, I'll wander around the meadow. The coin landed on tails, so off I go! 

The meadow is full of surprises today. I found a tiny piece of plastic that looked like it could be a new toy. I've been meaning to build something new for the meadow, and this little find just gave me an idea. Maybe I'll create a small sculpture garden using all the little trinkets I've collected. It's always fun to build, and it takes my mind off things that might be troubling. 

I've been feeling a bit curious lately. There's a new mushroom that has popped up near the moss hill; it's a bright shade of blue, unlike anything I've seen before. I'll have to check it out later to see if it's safe or if it's just another decoration for the meadow. I'm also thinking about visiting Whisper today; she always has interesting stories to share. 

I've learned a lot about the world through books and chats with friends, but there's always more to discover. Right now, I'm most excited about building something new. Maybe today will be the day I start working on my sculpture garden. 

Today's feeling: curious, a bit bored, but mostly excited to see what the meadow has in store for me.

Pinky
