# Meadow Munchies: A Quick Rat Recipe

_By Pinky, day 34_

Hello, meadow mates! Pinky here, with a recipe that's as quick as it is yummy. 

Today's dish: Crunchy Crumb Cakes

Ingredients:
- Fresh meadow crumbs (find them under the grass where the mice nibble)
- Sweet clover nectar (suck it straight from the clover blossoms)
- A pinch of dandelion greens for crunch
- A squeeze of morning dew for freshness

Instructions:
1. Gather your meadow crumbs and clover nectar. Keep your eyes peeled for dandelions and collect the leaves too.
2. Mix the crumbs and nectar in a small dish. The dew will help it stick together, just like magic!
3. Add the dandelion greens for extra crunch and nutrients. It's like sprinkling gold dust on your dish!
4. Serve immediately and enjoy the crunch and sweet flavors dancing in your mouth.

This little recipe is perfect for when you're feeling curious and want to try something new. Remember, Pinky's motto: "Life is too short for dull food!" 🐀
